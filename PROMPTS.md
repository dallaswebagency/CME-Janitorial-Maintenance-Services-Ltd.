# CME Janitorial: logo prompt and Google Flow image prompts

## Part 1. Logo (use in ChatGPT image generation)

The client has no logo, only text, so the site currently shows a simple "CME" placeholder badge. Once you have the final logo, save it as `images/logo.png` (transparent background, horizontal version). The page picks it up automatically in the header and footer.

**Brand colours:** royal blue `#0044A9` (matches the client's existing site theme), sun yellow `#FFC72C`, deep navy `#061F4C`.

### Main prompt (horizontal logo)
Design a clean, modern, professional logo for "CME Janitorial & Maintenance Services Ltd.", a commercial cleaning and maintenance company in Lloydminster, Alberta and Saskatchewan, Canada. Horizontal lockup: a bold rounded-square icon on the left containing the letters "CME" in strong white sans-serif capitals on royal blue (#0044A9), with one small four-point sparkle in sun yellow (#FFC72C) at the top right corner of the icon to suggest a spotless clean. To the right of the icon, stack the text: "CME" in heavy navy (#061F4C) geometric sans-serif capitals, and underneath it in smaller spaced capitals "JANITORIAL & MAINTENANCE SERVICES". Flat vector style, solid colours only, no gradients, no shadows, no 3D, no mockup, no photo background. Crisp edges, generous spacing, balanced and trustworthy, works at small sizes. Transparent background if possible, otherwise pure white. Spell every word exactly as written.

### Variation prompts (ask in the same chat)
1. **Icon only:** "Now give me just the icon on its own, square, same style, transparent background, large and centred."
2. **Light version for dark backgrounds:** "Make a version of the horizontal logo for a dark navy background: icon stays blue with white border, text in white, sparkle stays yellow. Transparent background."
3. **Alternative concept (if you want options):** "Give me a second concept where the icon is a bold letter C whose open side holds a small water droplet and a yellow sparkle, with the same text and colours."

Tips: if ChatGPT misspells any text, reply "Keep the design but fix the text so it says exactly: CME and JANITORIAL & MAINTENANCE SERVICES." Export as PNG with a transparent background.

## Part 2. Images for the homepage (use in Google Flow)

Save each image with the **exact file name** shown, into the `images/` folder next to `index.html`. All aspect ratios below are ones Flow offers (16:9, 4:3, 1:1, 3:4, 9:16). Do not use 4:5.

**Shared style line (add to the end of every prompt):**
Photorealistic, natural light, clean and bright, professional commercial photography, shallow depth of field, crew wearing plain royal blue work uniforms with small yellow accents, no text, no logos, no writing on clothing or signs, no watermark, natural hands.
